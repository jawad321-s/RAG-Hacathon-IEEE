The Inorganic Chemistry Lab is an advanced course in inorganic chemistry designed to introduce fundamental principles, including the synthesis and reactions of coordination compounds. Purification techniques, identification methods, and the chemical and physical characteristics of the synthesized compounds will be explored. Additionally, basic concepts of valence bond theory and crystal field theory, along with their applications in describing bonding, reactivity, isomers, and spectroscopy of coordination compounds, will be covered.
