The course covers the following topics: 1. Introduction to the Islamic system, providing a general overview of the objectives of Islamic law (Sharia), and defining the characteristics of the Islamic system. 2. The political system in Islam, discussing the concept of the state and its pillars, the concept of Dar al-Islam, the goals of the Islamic state, international relations in Islam, and the supreme constitutional principles in Islamic legislation. 7. The system of punishments in Islam, discussing the definition of crime and punishment and their characteristics, including hudud (fixed punishments), qisas (retributive justice), and ta'zir (discretionary punishments).
