## صفحة 3

This course addresses the nature of constitutions and constitutional law by clarifying the nature of constitutional law and its relationship with other branches of law, as well as identifying the sources of constitutional legal rules. It also explains the meaning of a constitution and its various types, then examines their origins and termination, and highlights the relative decline in the value of constitutions and how their supremacy can be preserved—through mechanisms of oversight and their application, whether political or judicial. The course further explores systems of government, outlining their diverse constitutional and political foundations, and clarifies the concept of democratic government and its various forms. It then presents examples of systems of governance applied in the contemporary world, discusses the Palestinian constitutional system, and compares all of this with the perspective of Islamic law on constitutional matters.
