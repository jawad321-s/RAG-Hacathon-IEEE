Part II. Infectious Diseases: An overview will be given of critical topics related to the identification, control and prevention of newly emerging and as well as long recognized infectious diseases with public health importance. The emphasis will be on major infections that are prevalent in tropical and developing countries. The clinical presentation, modes of transmission, geographic distribution and prevention of these infections will be considered from an epidemiologic perspective. Selected global disease control programs and policies will be assessed for their strengths and weaknesses
